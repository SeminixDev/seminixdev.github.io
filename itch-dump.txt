 [2020/Solo] Spectral Ninja - 2D fighter platformer featuring a time rewind mechanic.
 https://seminix.itch.io/spectral-ninja / https://www.newgrounds.com/portal/view/764843
 Source code available: https://github.com/SeminixDev/Spectral-Ninja
Brackeys Jam 2020.2 - Theme: Rewind
Analytics: 285 views, 152 browser plays. Out of 1773 entires, ranked 331 overall, 267 fun.
Also published on newgrounds - 1,061 views, 89 ratings 3.19/5.00
Released: August 8th 2020
Last updated: Jun 13, 2021
Unity C#
You control a ninja capable of killing enemies through the spectral dimension.
Controls:
[A] [D] - Move left and right
[Space] Jump
[L] Rewind (four seconds)
[K] Dash (immune to damage)
[J] Attack
[J] [J] Double Attack
[W] Go through door
Alternative controls are also available and shown in the tutorial
Instructions and help:
You must kill at least half of the enemies to open a door that leads to the next level. Rewinding will restore 50% of the health you lost during the last four seconds.
You do physical (normal) damage with your attacks. Your second attack and dash also do spectral damage (marked blue on the enemies' healthbar). Spectral damage can be triggered by rewinding.
